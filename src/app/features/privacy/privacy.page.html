Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We keep only what you type into a game: your nickname and your moves.
  • A room closes 24 hours after it is created.
  • Your phone remembers which rooms you are in, in local storage. No cookies.

What we store on our server

When you create or join a room, the room's record in our database holds:

  • the nickname you enter;
  • what you enter while playing: questions, answers, guesses and picks;
  • scores and the history of the rounds;
  • a hash of your player token, never the token itself;
  • the room password, if the host set one, only as a salted hash;
  • when each player was last seen online.

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

How long we keep it

A room closes 24 hours after it is created. After that nobody can join it, read it or play in it through SenReveal. The closed record currently stays in the database; it is not shown to anyone, used for anything else or shared. To have it deleted, write to us with the room code and your nickname.

What stays on your device

For every room you join, the app keeps the room code, your player ID, your player token and your nickname in your browser's local storage, plus the last nickname you used, to fill it in next time. That is how the app knows it is you when you come back. Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app removes it; you can then join again as a new player.

Who can see what you write

The other players in your room see your nickname, and see your answers when the round reveals them — that is the game. Anyone who has the six-digit code (and the password, if there is one) can join while the room is open, so do not write anything you would not say out loud in that room.

Services we rely on

  • Vercel hosts the site and the game server. Like any web host it processes your IP address and keeps short-lived request logs. We use the IP address only to limit how fast one device can make requests.
  • Supabase hosts the database with the room records described above.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts. Your browser fetches them from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

The Android app

The SenReveal app on Google Play opens this same website in Chrome, as a trusted web activity. Everything on this page applies to it unchanged. It asks for no extra permissions beyond internet access.

Children

SenReveal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
