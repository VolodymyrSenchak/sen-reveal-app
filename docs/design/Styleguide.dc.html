Rules that hold it together

One primary button per screen. It is always lime, always at the bottom, always the thing the round is waiting for.

Buttons follow the server, not the client. Every action button maps to a can* flag in the pushed state — show it when the flag is true, hide it otherwise, never re-derive the rules on the phone.

Everything is thumb-reachable. Controls are 44px minimum, primary actions 56–62px, and the bottom third of the screen belongs to whatever comes next.

Secrecy is visible. A lock line sits on every screen where something is hidden, so people trust that nobody — asker included — is reading ahead.

Reveals land, they don't slide. Cards flip in on reveal, 180ms, one after another; everything else moves in 120ms or not at all.

Dark by default. This gets played at night with the lights low — no light mode planned, and no white flashes between screens.
